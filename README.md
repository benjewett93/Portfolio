# Portfolio
My professional portfolio to showcase my work as a digital marketing specialist.

A fast, dependency-free static site: `index.html`, `styles.css` and `script.js`. It's responsive, supports dark mode and respects reduced-motion settings.

## View it locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `python3 -m http.server` and visit http://localhost:8000.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root. The site will be live at `https://benjewett93.github.io/Portfolio/`.

## Make it yours
Every spot that needs your real details is marked with a `REPLACE:` comment in `index.html`:

- **Hero stats**: years of experience, campaigns launched, average ROAS.
- **Case studies**: the three projects are sample content. Swap in your real (or anonymized) clients, goals, tactics and results.
- **About**: your bio, plus a headshot at `assets/headshot.jpg`.
- **Contact**: your email address and LinkedIn URL.

Colors and fonts are tokens at the top of `styles.css` (`--accent`, `--font-display`, and so on).
